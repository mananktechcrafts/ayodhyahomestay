Local Events & Festivals

Cultural Heritage

Purulia Chau Dance

Witness the world-famous UNESCO recognized mask dance performed during local festivals and special evenings.

Seasonal - January

Tusu Festival

Experience the vibrant folk songs and local fairs (Mela) celebrated with great joy during the harvest season.

Seasonal - March

Palash Utsav

Watch the hills turn fiery red with Palash flowers as the spring festival marks the arrival of Holi.

Local Market & Handicrafts

🎭 Charida Chhau Masks

Visit Charida village nearby, world-famous for handcrafted masks used in Chhau dance. Perfect for home decor souvenirs.

👜 Babui Grass Items

Discover beautiful bags, mats, and baskets hand-woven by local tribal women using natural Babui grass.

🍯 Forest Honey

Purchase 100% pure, organic wild forest honey collected from the deep woods of the Ayodhya range.

Best Time to Visit

October - February (Winter)

The ideal time for trekking and sightseeing. Cool weather and clear skies make it perfect for exploring Ayodhya Hills.

March - April (Spring)

Witness the Palash Festival. The entire landscape turns fiery red with Palash blooms—a photographer's paradise.

July - September (Monsoon)

Best for nature lovers. The hills become lush green and waterfalls like Bamni are at their peak beauty.
